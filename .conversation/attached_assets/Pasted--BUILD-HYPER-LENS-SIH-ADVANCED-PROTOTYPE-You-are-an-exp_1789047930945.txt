# 2. PROJECT PURPOSE

HYPER LENS is an AI-powered Legal Metrology compliance platform.

It helps:

* Manufacturers
* Retailers
* E-commerce sellers
* Packaging companies
* Compliance officers
* Small businesses

scan product packaging and identify potentially missing or incorrect mandatory declarations.

The system should:

1. Scan product packaging.
2. Extract visible text using OCR.
3. Identify relevant declarations.
4. Compare them against configurable compliance rules.
5. Detect missing or potentially problematic information.
6. Generate a compliance score.
7. Explain issues in simple language.
8. Recommend corrective actions.
9. Generate a professional compliance report.

IMPORTANT:

This is an **AI-assisted preliminary assessment**, not legal certification.
